# 001 — Colors in the Garden

**YouTube title:** Can You Find All the Colors? 🌈 | Play and Learn

## Overview

A 60-second vertical educational video designed to introduce young children to four basic colors: **red, yellow, blue, and green**.

The story takes place in a peaceful garden. Lily, a kind white bunny wearing soft pink clothes, and Pip, a small friendly yellow bird with a tiny blue scarf, invite children to identify objects, repeat each color aloud, and review all four colors together.

## Learning objectives

- Recognize the colors red, yellow, blue, and green.
- Associate each color with a familiar object.
- Practice listening and repeating English color words.
- Participate by answering simple questions aloud.

## Creative and production requirements

- Use gentle lighting, pastel colors, and slow camera movement.
- Include character dialogue in every scene.
- Keep all spoken dialogue and on-screen text in English.
- Use large, clear subtitles matching the spoken words.
- Include short pauses so children can repeat each color.
- Avoid frightening imagery, danger, violence, loud sounds, fast camera movement, flashing effects, logos, and copyrighted characters.
- End at exactly 60 seconds without additional scenes or dialogue.
