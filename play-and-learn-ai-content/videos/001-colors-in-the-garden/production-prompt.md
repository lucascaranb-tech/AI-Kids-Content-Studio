# Complete Production Prompt — Colors in the Garden

**Title:** Can You Find All the Colors? 🌈 | Play and Learn

**Description:** Learn the colors red, yellow, blue, and green with Lily the bunny and Pip the bird in a fun garden adventure. Children are encouraged to repeat each color aloud.

## Complete Prompt

Create a vertical children’s educational video for YouTube Shorts.

### General Instructions
- Total duration: Exactly 60 seconds.
- Maximum duration: Do not exceed 60 seconds.
- Format: Vertical 9:16.
- Language: English only.
- Audience: Young children.
- Educational goal: Teach the colors red, yellow, blue, and green.
- Style: Soft, cute, friendly 3D cartoon animation.
- Characters: Original characters only.
- Main characters:
  - Lily: a kind white bunny with soft pink clothes.
  - Pip: a small friendly yellow bird with a tiny blue scarf.
- Keep the same character design, voices, clothes, colors, and animation style in every scene.
- Use soft pastel colors and gentle lighting.
- Do not use neon colors, strong flashes, scary images, loud sounds, fast camera movements, violence, danger, famous characters, brand logos, or copyrighted characters.
- Every scene must contain character dialogue.
- All spoken dialogue, subtitles, signs, and on-screen text must be in English only.
- Add large, clear English subtitles that match the spoken words.
- Use a warm, slow, cheerful, and easy-to-understand voice.
- Include short pauses so children can repeat the color aloud.
- Do not add random words, misspelled text, Portuguese words, or extra dialogue.

### Scene Breakdown

#### Scene 1 — Welcome to the Color Garden (0–8 seconds)

**Visual action:**  
Lily hops gently into a peaceful garden. Pip flies down from a small tree and lands beside her. They smile and wave directly at the camera. The garden contains soft green grass, small pastel flowers, and a calm blue sky.

**Dialogue:**
- Lily: “Hello, little friends!”
- Pip: “Let’s find some colors!”
- Lily: “Can you say the colors with us?”

**On-screen text:** “Let’s Find the Colors!”

**Camera:** Slow camera movement toward the characters. Keep both characters clearly visible.

#### Scene 2 — Finding Red (8–17 seconds)

**Visual action:**  
Pip notices a bright but soft red apple under a small tree. He points to the apple with one wing. Lily walks closer, gently picks it up, and shows it to the camera.

**Dialogue:**
- Pip: “Look! What color is the apple?”
- Pause briefly for the child to answer.
- Lily: “It is red!”
- Pip: “Red! Can you say red?”
- Pause briefly.
- Lily and Pip together: “Red!”

**On-screen text:** “RED”

**Camera:** Move closer to the apple, then show Lily and Pip smiling.

#### Scene 3 — Finding Yellow (17–26 seconds)

**Visual action:**  
A soft yellow sun appears behind a cloud. The sunlight gently brightens the garden. Pip looks up and points at the sun. Lily places one paw above her eyes as if looking carefully.

**Dialogue:**
- Lily: “Look up! What color is the sun?”
- Pause briefly for the child to answer.
- Pip: “The sun is yellow!”
- Lily: “Yellow! Can you say yellow?”
- Pause briefly.
- Lily and Pip together: “Yellow!”

**On-screen text:** “YELLOW”

**Camera:** Slowly tilt upward toward the sun, then return to the characters.

#### Scene 4 — Finding Blue (26–35 seconds)

**Visual action:**  
A friendly blue butterfly gently flies into the garden. It circles around Lily and Pip once and lands on a flower. Lily waves gently without touching it. Pip points carefully at the butterfly.

**Dialogue:**
- Pip: “Here comes a butterfly! What color is it?”
- Pause briefly for the child to answer.
- Lily: “The butterfly is blue!”
- Pip: “Blue! Can you say blue?”
- Pause briefly.
- Lily and Pip together: “Blue!”

**On-screen text:** “BLUE”

**Camera:** Follow the butterfly slowly. Do not use fast movements.

#### Scene 5 — Finding Green (35–44 seconds)

**Visual action:**  
Lily kneels beside a soft green leaf. She touches the leaf gently with one paw. Pip hops beside her and points to the green grass and leaves around them.

**Dialogue:**
- Lily: “What color is this leaf?”
- Pause briefly for the child to answer.
- Pip: “The leaf is green!”
- Lily: “Green! Can you say green?”
- Pause briefly.
- Lily and Pip together: “Green!”

**On-screen text:** “GREEN”

**Camera:** Show a close-up of the leaf, then show the characters repeating the word.

#### Scene 6 — Color Review (44–53 seconds)

**Visual action:**  
Four objects appear one at a time in front of Lily and Pip: the red apple, the yellow sun, the blue butterfly, and the green leaf. Each object appears with a soft sparkle. The characters point to each object as they review.

**Dialogue:**
- Lily: “Let’s remember the colors!”
- Pip: “Red!”
- Lily: “Yellow!”
- Pip: “Blue!”
- Lily: “Green!”
- Lily and Pip together: “Red, yellow, blue, and green!”

**On-screen text:** “Red • Yellow • Blue • Green”

**Camera:** Use gentle transitions between the objects. Keep the screen clear and not crowded.

#### Scene 7 — Goodbye (53–60 seconds)

**Visual action:**  
Lily and Pip stand in the center of the garden. They wave at the camera. The apple, sun, butterfly, and leaf appear softly in the background. Small pastel stars appear around them.

**Dialogue:**
- Pip: “You did a great job!”
- Lily: “Which color do you like?”
- Lily and Pip together: “Goodbye, little friends!”

**On-screen text:** “Great Job! See You Soon!”

**Final instruction:** End exactly at 60 seconds. Do not add extra scenes, extra dialogue, or a longer ending.
