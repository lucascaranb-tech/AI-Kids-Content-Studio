# Complete Production Prompt — Count from 1 to 5

Create a children's educational video for the YouTube Shorts channel "PLAY AND LEARN".

## Video Requirements
- Total duration: exactly 60 seconds.
- Maximum duration: 60 seconds. Do not exceed this limit.
- Format: vertical 9:16.
- Number of scenes: 6.
- Each scene must last exactly 10 seconds.
- Language: English only.
- All spoken dialogue, narration, subtitles, signs, labels, and on-screen text must be in English.
- Educational goal: teach young children to count from 1 to 5.
- Every scene must include clear character dialogue. Buddy and Mimi must speak in every scene.
- The characters must speak directly to the children and encourage them to answer aloud.
- Keep the dialogue simple, slow, cheerful, and easy for young children to repeat.
- Use short pauses after questions so children can respond, but keep each scene within 10 seconds.
- Prioritize the characters' voices and dialogue over complicated visual actions.

## Main Characters

### Buddy
A friendly original golden-brown puppy with floppy ears, a small blue collar, big gentle eyes, and a cheerful personality. Buddy is energetic, encouraging, and speaks clearly.

### Mimi
A friendly original gray-and-white kitten with soft fur, a small yellow bow, big expressive eyes, and a calm, curious personality. Mimi is patient and helps children repeat the numbers.

Keep Buddy and Mimi visually consistent in every scene. Do not change their fur colors, clothing, accessories, size, voices, or personality.

## Visual Style
- Soft 3D children's animation.
- Warm, cozy, safe, and familiar home environment.
- Use gentle pastel colors and soft lighting.
- Avoid neon colors, strong contrast, flashing lights, rapid camera movements, visual clutter, and excessive effects.
- Use simple objects that are easy for children to identify.
- No scary elements, danger, violence, loud noises, strange objects, or sudden movements.
- Do not use famous characters, copyrighted characters, logos, brands, or recognizable existing media designs.
- The characters should look directly at the camera whenever they speak to the children.
- Use large, clear English subtitles that match the spoken dialogue exactly.
- Display each number clearly on screen when it is spoken.

## Scene 1 — Living Room (0–10 seconds)

**Setting:** A cozy family living room with a soft rug, a small sofa, cushions, and a basket containing colorful but softly toned toys. Buddy and Mimi sit on the rug facing the camera.

**Actions:** Buddy waves with one paw and smiles directly at the children. Mimi points gently toward the toy basket. Buddy picks up one soft ball and holds it where the children can see it. Both characters look at the camera while speaking.

**Dialogue:**
- BUDDY: “Hello, little friends!”
- MIMI: “Let's learn to count!”
- BUDDY: “Can you count one ball?”
- MIMI: “Say it with us: one!”

**On-screen text:** “LET'S COUNT!” and “1 — ONE”

**Important:** The number “1” and the word “ONE” must be large, centered, and easy to read.

## Scene 2 — Bedroom (10–20 seconds)

**Setting:** A warm children's bedroom with a neatly made bed, soft pillows, a small bookshelf, and two plush stars on the bed.

**Actions:** Mimi gently places two plush stars side by side on the bed. Buddy points to each star slowly, one at a time. The characters pause briefly after asking the question and look directly at the camera to invite the children to answer.

**Dialogue:**
- MIMI: “Look! How many stars do you see?”
- BUDDY: “Let's count them together.”
- BUDDY: “One…”
- MIMI: “Two!”
- BUDDY AND MIMI: “Two stars!”

**On-screen text:** “2 — TWO” and “TWO STARS”

**Important:** Show the number “2” and the word “TWO” clearly while the characters count.

## Scene 3 — Kitchen (20–30 seconds)

**Setting:** A safe, clean family kitchen with a small table, two soft-colored cups, and a bowl of fruit. Do not show sharp objects, hot surfaces, or dangerous items.

**Actions:** Buddy stands beside the table and points to two cups. Mimi gently places a third cup next to them. Buddy and Mimi count the cups together, touching or pointing to each cup in order. They face the camera after counting.

**Dialogue:**
- BUDDY: “Can you help us count the cups?”
- MIMI: “One…”
- BUDDY: “Two…”
- MIMI: “Three!”
- BUDDY AND MIMI: “Three cups!”

**On-screen text:** “3 — THREE” and “THREE CUPS”

**Important:** The three cups must remain visible and clearly separated. Make the counting action slow and easy to follow.

## Scene 4 — Playroom (30–40 seconds)

**Setting:** A bright but calm playroom with a soft carpet. Place exactly four identical soft-colored cushions in a straight line on the carpet, spaced apart. Nothing else is on the floor.

**Actions:** Buddy stands at the left end of the line of cushions, facing the camera. Mimi sits at the right end. Buddy points to the first cushion and says “One!” Mimi points to the second cushion and says “Two!” Buddy points to the third cushion and says “Three!” Mimi points to the fourth cushion and says “Four!” Each cushion is pointed to only once, from left to right, in this exact order. After the fourth number, both characters turn to the camera and smile.

**Dialogue:**
- BUDDY: “Let's count the cushions with me!”
- BUDDY: “One!”
- MIMI: “Two!”
- BUDDY: “Three!”
- MIMI: “Four!”
- BUDDY AND MIMI: “We have four cushions!”
- MIMI: “Now you count with us. How many cushions?”
- BUDDY AND MIMI: “Four!”

**On-screen text:** “4 — FOUR” and “FOUR CUSHIONS”

**Important:** Exactly four cushions must be visible for the whole scene. Show the number “4” and the word “FOUR” each time the number is spoken. Pause briefly after “How many cushions?” so the children can answer aloud.

## Scene 5 — Dining Room (40–50 seconds)

**Setting:** A cozy dining room with a small wooden table. Place exactly five identical soft-colored toy apples in a straight line on the table, spaced apart. Nothing else is on the table.

**Actions:** Mimi sits at the left end of the table, facing the camera. Buddy sits at the right end. Mimi points to the apples slowly from left to right and says one number for each apple. Buddy nods after each number. After the fifth apple, Buddy turns to the camera and invites the children to count with them. Keep the camera steady and show all five apples clearly for the whole scene. Do not add, remove, hide, or move any apple.

**Dialogue:**
- MIMI: “Let's count the apples!”
- MIMI: “One!”
- MIMI: “Two!”
- MIMI: “Three!”
- MIMI: “Four!”
- MIMI: “Five!”
- BUDDY: “We have five apples!”
- BUDDY: “Now you count with us. Let's say it together!”
- BUDDY AND MIMI: “One, two, three, four, five!”

**On-screen text:** “5 — FIVE” and “FIVE APPLES”

**Important:**
- All five apples must be clearly visible at the same time.
- The word “FIVE” must be large and easy to read.
- There must be exactly five apples on screen.
- The numbers must be said in this exact order: one, two, three, four, five.
- Each number must match one apple, and each apple must be pointed to only once.
- Do not skip, repeat, or add any number.
- Pause briefly after “Now you count with us” so the children can answer aloud.

## Scene 6 — Goodbye (50–60 seconds)

**Setting:** The same cozy living room from Scene 1. Buddy and Mimi sit together on the soft rug. The background is simple, with no toys, numbers, cards, or extra objects on screen.

**Actions:** Buddy and Mimi face the camera and smile. Buddy waves with one paw. Mimi waves with one paw after him. Keep the camera steady and centered on both characters. Do not add any movement that could distract the children.

**Dialogue:**
- BUDDY: “Goodbye, little friends!”
- MIMI: “Thank you for counting with us!”
- BUDDY AND MIMI: “See you next time at PLAY AND LEARN! Bye, bye!”

**On-screen text:** “GOODBYE!” and “PLAY AND LEARN”

**Important:** Do not include any counting or numbers in Scene 6.

## Final Technical Instructions
- End the video exactly at 60 seconds.
- Do not add an extra ending, logo animation, credits, or additional scene.
- Keep the characters' voices clear and louder than the background music.
- Use gentle, cheerful background music at a low volume.
- Make sure every spoken word is in English.
- Make sure every subtitle and on-screen word is in English.
- Do not generate Portuguese text or speech.
- Do not add unplanned dialogue.
- Do not skip any scene.
- Do not merge scenes.
- Do not make any scene longer or shorter than 10 seconds.
- Every scene must have spoken dialogue from Buddy, Mimi, or both.
- The final video must clearly teach children to count from 1 to 5 through repetition and direct interaction.
