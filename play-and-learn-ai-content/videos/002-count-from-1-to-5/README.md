# 002 — Count from 1 to 5 with Buddy and Mimi

**Suggested YouTube title:** Let's Count from 1 to 5 with Buddy and Mimi! | PLAY AND LEARN

## Overview

A 60-second vertical educational video designed to help young children count from **one to five** in English.

Buddy, a friendly golden-brown puppy with floppy ears and a small blue collar, and Mimi, a friendly gray-and-white kitten with a small yellow bow, guide children through counting familiar objects in different rooms of a cozy home.

## Learning objectives

- Practice counting from one to five in the correct order.
- Match spoken numbers to visible objects.
- Listen to and repeat simple English number words.
- Participate by answering questions and counting aloud.

- **Buddy:** an original golden-brown puppy with floppy ears, a small blue collar, big gentle eyes, and an energetic, encouraging personality.
- **Mimi:** an original gray-and-white kitten with soft fur, a small yellow bow, big expressive eyes, and a calm, curious personality.

The prompt requires consistent character appearance, accessories, voices, and personalities throughout all scenes.

## Scene outline

| Scene | Time | Counting focus |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Living Room | 0–10s | One ball |
| 2. Bedroom | 10–20s | Two plush stars |
| 3. Kitchen | 20–30s | Three cups |
| 4. Playroom | 30–40s | Four cushions |
| 5. Dining Room | 40–50s | Five toy apples |
| 6. Goodbye | 50–60s | Farewell; no counting |

## Creative and production requirements

- Use a warm, cozy, safe home environment with gentle pastel colors and soft lighting.
- Buddy and Mimi must speak in every scene and address the children directly.
- Prioritize clear character dialogue over complicated visual actions.
- Use large English subtitles matching the spoken dialogue.
- Display each number clearly when it is spoken in counting scenes.
- Keep objects clearly visible and countable; follow the exact quantities and counting order in the prompt.
- Avoid visual clutter, rapid camera movements, flashing lights, frightening elements, danger, violence, logos, and copyrighted characters.
- Keep each scene exactly 10 seconds and end at exactly 60 seconds.
- Scene 6 must not include counting or numbers.
